GET TO KNOW ME

Every face has a story. Every character has a soul.

I'm Surya Deva Bollepalli, an actor with a passion for cinema and a strong desire to explore the different layers of human emotions.

My journey as an actor includes training at Mayukha, performing in stage plays at Bhoomika Theatre, and training at Actor's Truth in Mumbai. These experiences have helped me understand myself better, observe people more deeply, and explore the honesty and vulnerability that acting demands.
